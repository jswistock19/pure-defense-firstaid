THE SCIENCE

How Pure Defense Works

Patented Water Life Science® technology delivers ultra-pure, sterile water through a precision aerosol system — safe for skin, eyes, and mucous membranes.

01 — Apply

Spray Pure Defense directly on wounds, burns, or irritated skin. The sterile mist cleanses without burning or stinging.

02 — Irrigate

For eye emergencies, hold can 8-12 inches away. The natural water floods and flushes irritants — gasoline, tear gas, OC pepper spray, chemicals.

03 — Protect

Apply barrier moisturizing ointment after cleansing. Pure Defense leaves zero residue — no formulated ingredients, no adverse reactions.
