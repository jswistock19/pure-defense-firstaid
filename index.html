THE SCIENCE

How Pure Defense Works

Patented Water Life Science® technology delivers ultra-pure, sterile water through a precision aerosol system — safe for skin, eyes, and mucous membranes.

01 — Apply

Spray Pure Defense directly on wounds, burns, or irritated skin. The sterile mist cleanses without burning or stinging.

02 — Irrigate

For eye emergencies, hold can 8-12 inches away. The natural water floods and flushes irritants — gasoline, tear gas, OC pepper spray, chemicals.

03 — Protect

Apply barrier moisturizing ointment after cleansing. Pure Defense leaves zero residue — no formulated ingredients, no adverse reactions.

FAQ

Common Questions

Pure Defense is 100% natural sterile water — no saline, no formulated ingredients, no chemicals. It will not burn or sting on open wounds, in the eyes, or on mucous membranes. Standard wound washes contain saline and preservatives that can cause irritation.

Yes. Pure Defense is safe for use as an emergency eye irrigant. Hold the can 8-12 inches from the face and aim water into and around the eye. Continue flushing until all irritants are removed. If solid particles remain, cover with a sterile pad and seek medical attention.

Pure Defense has a 4-year sterile shelf life from the date of manufacture. The pressurized container maintains sterility throughout its entire shelf life without preservatives.

Pure Defense is manufactured in an FDA-registered facility (Global Marketing/Licensing Bio-Logic Aqua® Inc.) in Grants Pass, Oregon with current FDA product and packaging facility registration and FDA product export license.
